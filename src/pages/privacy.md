# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 24 July 2026**

This policy covers **eigeninteractive.com**, this website, where we publish the
EigenInteractive open-source game engine, its documentation, and the games we
build with it. It is deliberately short, because the site collects very little.

This policy does **not** cover the games themselves. Each game is a separate app
with its own privacy policy, shown inside the app and on the game's own website.

## What this site collects

- **Privacy-first analytics.** We use [Cloudflare Web Analytics](https://www.cloudflare.com/web-analytics/)
  to understand which pages are read and how the site performs. It is
  cookieless: it sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser, does not
  track you across sites, and does not collect or store your IP address or any
  personal information. It cannot be used to identify you.
- **Server logs.** Our hosting provider (Cloudflare) generates standard request
  logs, used to keep the site secure and available.

We do not use advertising trackers, we do not sell data, and there is no sign-in
or account on this site.

## Cookies

This site sets no tracking cookies. Your browser's own theme preference (light or
dark) may be stored locally on your device; it never leaves your browser.

## Third parties

The site is served by Cloudflare, which processes requests and provides the
analytics described above under its own privacy terms. Documentation pages may
link to external sites (GitHub, pub.dev, and similar); those services have their
own privacy policies.

## Changes

If we add anything that collects more data, we will update this page and revise
the date above.
